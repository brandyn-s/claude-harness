## Round 4 — MAIN (resolve disagreements + new findings)

You now have access to all agents' Round 3 main outputs and your own Round 4 pre-reg.

### Your task — three parts

#### Part A — Genuine remaining disagreements

For each disagreement that genuinely survived Round 3:
- The claim under dispute (one sentence)
- Disputing positions (which agent says what, with quoted citation if helpful)
- **Resolution path**: EXPERIMENT (specific test) | EVIDENCE NEEDED (specific source) | AGREE TO DISAGREE (with reasons)
- **Falsifier**: what observation would convince you the disagreement is actually resolved?

Things that ARE NOT genuine disagreements: style/framing differences, claims fully resolved by Round 3 concessions, artifacts of who saw what context.

#### Part B — Pre-reg comparison

Compare your Round 4 pre-reg against actual Round 3:
- Where did your prediction match? (calibration check)
- Where did your prediction miss? (which way — over- or under-predicted concession)
- What does this say about your model of the other agents?

#### Part C — New findings from cross-talk

Up to 3 findings that no one stated in Round 1 but became visible only because the agents engaged with each other.
- The finding
- Which exchange surfaced it
- Confidence (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW) + falsifier

### Output format

```
## Genuine remaining disagreements

### Disagreement 1: [one-line summary]
**Positions**: [who says what]
**Resolution**: [EXPERIMENT | EVIDENCE NEEDED | AGREE TO DISAGREE]
**Falsifier**: [specific observation]

[...]

## Pre-reg comparison
[2-3 paragraphs]

## New findings from cross-talk
1. [finding] — surfaced in [exchange] — [confidence + falsifier]
[...up to 3...]
```

Length: 500-1000 words. Be ruthless about what counts as "genuine."
