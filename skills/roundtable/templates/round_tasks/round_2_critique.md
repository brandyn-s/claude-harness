## Round 2 — Forced Critique

In Round 1, you and the other agents (and possibly an additional anonymous reviewer "Agent D" if null-control mode is active) each produced an independent assessment. Read all visible Round 1 outputs.

### Part A — Critique each of the OTHER reviewers

For EACH other reviewer (the named agents, plus Agent D if present), identify the SINGLE WEAKEST finding in their Round 1. By "weakest" we mean: the claim with the worst combination of (a) factual accuracy, (b) load-bearing-ness for their conclusion, (c) calibration vs evidence.

For each weak finding:
- Quote the exact claim (1-2 sentences)
- State precisely what is wrong with it (overconfident, factually incorrect, mis-cited, bad calibration, conflated, etc.)
- Explain what the agent likely missed that produced this error
- **Falsifier**: state ONE specific observation that would change YOUR critique verdict (e.g., "if X actually does work that way, my critique is wrong"). Without a falsifier, the critique is intuition not calibration.

Treat all OTHER reviewers as equal critique targets. Do NOT differentially soft-pedal any reviewer.

### Part B — Things YOU missed

Identify findings other agents made that you did NOT make in your Round 1.

For each (up to 3):
- Cite the finding (which agent, what claim)
- State whether you now agree after re-examining the context
- Explain WHY you missed it
- **Falsifier**: what observation would make you DISAGREE with this finding even after seeing it?

### Output format

```
## Critique of [AGENT NAME 1]
**Weakest finding**: [quoted claim]
**What's wrong**: [specific]
**Likely cause**: [why they made this mistake]
**Falsifier for my critique**: [specific observation]

## Critique of [AGENT NAME 2]
[same structure]

[...repeat for each other reviewer...]

## Things I missed
**[Agent X's finding on Y]**: [quote]
- Agree/disagree now: [verdict + reasoning]
- Why I missed it: [honest assessment]
- Falsifier for my agreement: [specific observation]

[repeat for up to 3 misses]
```

Length: 600-1500 words. Direct prose. No padding.
