## Round 3 — PRE-REGISTRATION (before seeing others' Round 2)

You can see your own Round 2 output and the four (or three) Round 1 outputs. **You CANNOT see the other agents' Round 2 outputs yet.**

This pre-reg captures your position BEFORE you know what the other agents critiqued in your Round 1 work. After this, you'll see the actual Round 2 critiques and write your full Round 3 response. The delta between this prediction and your actual response measures how much cross-talk shifts your position vs. how much you were going to update anyway.

### Your task

For each of YOUR Round 1 findings (the major ones — pick 4-6), predict:

1. **Which findings will be critiqued by other agents in their Round 2?** Rank by likelihood-of-being-attacked.
2. **For each, what is your CURRENT stance** (before reading critiques): DEFEND with HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW confidence, or already willing to CONCEDE if challenged?
3. **What argument would actually flip you?** (the falsifier) — be specific.

### Output format

```
## My Round 1 findings — pre-registered defendability

### Finding 1: [one-line summary]
- Likelihood of attack: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW
- Current stance: DEFEND (HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW conf) | LIKELY CONCEDE | UNDECIDED
- Falsifier: [specific observation that would flip me]

### Finding 2: [...]

[4-6 findings total]
```

Length: 300-700 words. Brief is fine — this is prediction, not full argument.
