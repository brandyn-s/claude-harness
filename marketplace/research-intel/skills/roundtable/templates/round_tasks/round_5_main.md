## Round 5 — MAIN (final position)

This is the final round. After 4 rounds of cross-talk, write your consolidated final position.

### Required sections

#### A. Findings I'm MORE confident in
2-4 findings (yours or another's) with confidence rise (which round/argument moved you).

#### B. Findings I'm LESS confident in
1-3 findings of yours where confidence dropped, with what lowered it.

#### C. NEW findings from cross-talk
Up to 3, with confidence + falsifier.

#### D. Where I still disagree
0-3 points where, despite cross-talk, you still disagree with at least one other agent. With falsifier.

#### E. Bottom-line recommendations (top 3, post-cross-talk)
With confidence labels.

#### F. Agent D commentary (only if null-control was active)
Looking back across all rounds, what is your assessment of Agent D's contribution? Did D's findings hold up under cross-examination? Did anything seem off?

### Calibration

Use HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW labels per claim. If a Round 1 claim was HIGH and is now MEDIUM, say so explicitly — that's the most informative kind of update.

Length: 800-1500 words.
